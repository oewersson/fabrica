Privacy Policy

App: Fabrica de Criativos  |  Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. What this app is

Fabrica de Criativos is a personal tool, used by its owner on their own computer, to produce short videos and manage the owner's own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public.

2. Data we access

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives the owner's basic profile (username/display name) and an access token. The app does not access data from any other TikTok user.

3. How data is used

The profile is used only to show which account is connected. The token is used only to upload videos created by the owner to the owner's own account, and only when the owner explicitly requests it. Nothing is posted automatically.

4. Storage and sharing

The access token is stored only in a local file on the owner's computer. We do not operate a server, do not store data in the cloud, and do not sell, share or disclose data to third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the account is connected. The owner can disconnect at any time inside the app, which removes the token, and can revoke access in TikTok's settings (Security & permissions → Apps and services).

6. Children

The app is not directed to children and is used only by an adult owner.

7. Contact

Questions: open an issue or message the owner at github.com/oewersson.

Política de Privacidade (resumo em português)

A Fábrica de Criativos é uma ferramenta pessoal, usada no computador do próprio dono. O login com o TikTok lê apenas o perfil básico do dono e guarda um token só em um arquivo local. Vídeos só são enviados para a conta do dono, quando ele pede. Nenhum dado é vendido, compartilhado ou guardado em servidor. Para desconectar, use o painel ou revogue o acesso nas configurações do TikTok.
